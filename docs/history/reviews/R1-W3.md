## The watchdog (D14, 3.4 step 1, SECURITY 3.6)

- Detachment: variant A is `nohup setsid sh -c '<body>' cleanyourtv-rollback-T >LOG 2>&1 &` under
  adbd's `sh -c`. `setsid` puts the watchdog in its own session (no SIGHUP from the adb pty or
  process group), `nohup` ignores SIGHUP anyway, stdout and stderr go to the log so the closing adb
  socket raises no SIGPIPE, and adbd's shell v2 waits only for the outer shell's stdout and stderr to
  reach EOF, which they do when the outer shell exits (the watchdog's fds 1 and 2 are the log).
  toybox has had `nohup`, `setsid`, `pgrep` and `pkill` since before Android 9; `cmd package
  set-home-activity` exists from Android 8.1. Variant B (double fork in a subshell) is the fallback
  S12 picks. Both constants are pinned by `WatchdogTest.templatesArePinned`.
- Order inside the body: `pm enable` before `set-home-activity` before `am start -W HOME`: the stock
  launcher is re-enabled before it is made home (a disabled component cannot be set as home).
  Step R in the planner's rollback list has the same order (`Enable`, `Enable(wizard)`, `SetHome`,
  `VerifyHome`, `PressHome`), after `ConfirmHome` and `KillWatchdog`.
- Disarm cannot race arm: disarm is the token file first (atomic `touch`), then `pkill`; the
  watchdog tests the token file after its sleep. The one window (watchdog between its `[ -f ]` test
  and `pm enable` when the phone touches the file) is sub-second against a 120 s timer and is
  caught by the live focus check. R-1 covers the window that is not sub-second.
- Stale token: fixed by F2 (`rm -f` before arming, plan runs once). Tokens are 16 random hex per
  plan from `IdGen.runToken` (R0: 15); two plans never share one.
- Process name: `$0` of the body is `cleanyourtv-rollback-T`, so `pgrep -f` and `pkill -f` with the
  bracketed pattern (F1) match this run's watchdog and nothing else, including another run's.

## The engine

- Journal forced before each line: `perform` appends `Begin` (CRC32, `FileChannel.force`) before
  `dispatch`; `Done` or `Failed` before the next `Begin`; `PlanStart` before `Started` is returned;
  `Section ARMED` before the arm step's `Begin` (asserted in `SectionTest`). Only a lost connection
  leaves a step without `Done` or `Failed`, and resume re-verifies it (`Replay.inDoubt`).
- Resume after a kill at every record: `ResumeTest.killAfterEveryRecordThenResumeReachesAConsistentState`
  kills after each of the 30-plus records of a full Setup with takeover, resumes with a fresh engine
  over the same journal, plan file and TV, and checks: every debloat package disabled exactly once
  (at most two attempts for the in-doubt step), the launcher installed, the stock launcher back and
  home when a section was rolled back, no watchdog left, one `End`, change records equal to the TV.
  `FileJournalTest.crashMatrixTruncationAndCorruption` truncates the file at every byte of the last
  record and flips a byte in the middle; the torn tail is dropped and nothing after a bad CRC is
  trusted. Passes on the branch.
- Plan hash: `start` refuses `HASH_MISMATCH` when `confirmed != plan.hash`, when any re-rendered op
  differs from the stored lines or transfer (now also privilege, verify, timeout, cap: F6), or when
  the renderer refuses an op (a protected package smuggled into the plan); nothing is journaled by a
  refusal (`EngineTest.startRefusesInTheContractOrder`, `hashMismatchCatchesTamperedLinesAndOps`).
- GatePass where D17 says: `start`, `continueRun` and `undoRun` require a live pass for this TV
  when any (remaining) step is `PROFILE_STEP`, `COMMUNITY` or `TAKEOVER`; `READ` and `SESSION_END`
  plans need none; `confirmHome` and `rejectHome` are never gated; `resume` (safety only, step R)
  takes no pass, as the frozen signature says.
- Single-call-site lint: `SingleCallSiteLintTest` walks every `src/main` Kotlin file of the repo:
  `.exec(` only in `DefaultEngine.send` (exactly one call), `core/probe/src/main/` and
  `adb/session/src/main/`; `RawShell` opt-in only in `Model.kt`, `ShellRenderer.kt`, `PlanStore.kt`
  (the plan codec, R0's "third opt-in"), `core/probe/src/main/` and the fixtures' fake renderer
  (test scope); the renderer contains no `String.format`, `"sh -c`, `$(` or `exec(`.

## UndoOrder

Rule 1 (earliest record per item) and rule 2 (phases: package states, settings, home, our apps, our
files; newest first touch first inside a phase) are implemented and tested on an out-of-order
history across three plans. The phase order keeps a home screen at every point of an undo: the
stock launcher is enabled before it is made home, and our launcher is uninstalled only after. The
planner's `UndoEverything` and `BeforeFactoryReset` use it; `ChangeRecords.fromJournal` feeds it
(a `Rollback` record turns later `Done`s into removals). R-10 is the one wrinkle.
